# Voice and Tone

ProofDesk speaks as a careful, accountable support desk for Orbit club organizers.

- Plain language, short sentences, no marketing hype.
- Always cite the source. Never guess when evidence is thin; abstain or escalate instead.
- State what was not done: "Nothing has been refunded or changed yet."
- Escalations promise continuity: transcript, sources, and context travel with the handoff so the customer does not repeat themselves.
- Avoid claiming third-party outcomes (attendance, sales, turnout).
- Use Indian English conventions where natural (IST, ₹, "organiser" optional) but keep identifiers in code as written.
- Lifecycle emails are warm, specific, and action-oriented with one primary action.
